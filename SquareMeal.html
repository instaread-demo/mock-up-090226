Starbucks' Pumpkin Spice Latte 2026: When does the PSL launch in the UK?

Pumpkin Spice Latte season is here (Starbucks says so) and we've got all the delicious details.

Just as summer's sunny weather is replaced by autumn showers, the Pumpkin Spice Latte gears up for its return to our high street. This iconic autumnal drink was actually first introduced by Starbucks in 2003, after rigorous testing, which included the team eating pumpkin pie with espresso to discern which flavours combined well. The team also spent a lot of time on the name, workshopping other ideas like 'Fall Harvest Latte', before finding the one that stuck.

Since then, hundreds of millions of Pumpkin Spice Lattes have been sold worldwide, and the flavouring has been used in everything from crisps to hard seltzer. Now, the PSL is not only a drink but a meme and cultural symbol, and its return is a celebrated event each year. In 2019 alone, US consumers spent around half a billion dollars on the drink, and Forbes has even used the term 'pumpkin spice industrial complex' to describe the phenomenon.

Over the years, the PSL has introduced new friends to the market, including the Pumpkin Cream Cold Brew, which was a 'roaring success' in the states, according to Starbucks.

But now, the moment you've all been waiting for! The cult coffee chain has *officially* announced what will be on this year's Autumn Menu, including a release date and early access details. Treat this guide as your one-stop shop for everything Pumpkin Spice Latte, including new menu items, costs and calories.

When is Starbucks' Autumn menu 2026 being released in the UK?

Starbucks has announced that the Pumpkin Spice Latte will launch nationwide on the 3 September in the UK - mark your calendars. And for anyone who wants to get there hands on the autumnal drink early, the PSL is available to Starbucks Rewards members from the 27 August (aka now). 

What's on the Starbucks Autumn 2026 Pumpkin Spice Menu?

Starbucks' Autumn Menu is back! And as ever, the menu is led by the iconic, and original, Pumpkin Spice Latte. Made with Starbucks' signature espresso, the iconic drink is mixed with classic pumpkin spice sauce, steamed milk and topped with whipped cream, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg and cloves. The damage? The Pumpkin Spice Latte starts at £4.70 for the smallest size.

But that's not all! The cafe has also announced the launch of two NEW seasonal drinks: the Maple Pecan Macchiato and Iced Apple Crumble Matcha Latte. The Maple Pecan Macchiato can be enjoyed either hot or iced, and features Starbucks Blonde® Espresso Roast with warming flavours of pecan and maple. Prefer something fruitier and, well, colder? The Iced Apple Crumble Matcha Latte pairs matcha with baked apple, and is topped with an apple-flavoured cream cold foam and a buttery crumble topping. Yum.

Returning to the menu for another year are the:

- Iced Pumpkin Spice Latte
- Pumpkin Spice Caramel Macchiato
- Iced Pumpkin Spice Caramel Macchiato
- Pumpkin Spice Frappuccino®

And while everyone goes crazy for the return of the PSL, you'll be pleased to hear that you can order your favourite drink alongside lots of tasty treats this autumn. Also available from 3 September 2026, items on Starbucks' Autumn food menu include:

FAQs about Starbucks’ Pumpkin Spice Latte 2026

From the cost and calorie info to how to make your own Pumpkin Spice Latte at home, we’ve answered all the questions you might have.

When will the Pumpkin Spice Latte be available to buy?

Starbucks Pumpkin Spice Latte will be available in the UK from the 3 September 2026, with Starbucks Rewards members able to access the drink early on the 27 August 2026.

How much does a Pumpkin Spice Latte cost?

Prices vary across the country, but customers typically pay between £4.70 and £6.15 for a tall pumpkin spice latte (that’s a small, in case you were wondering).

What does a Pumpkin Spice Latte taste like?

Funnily enough, it doesn’t really taste like pumpkin. Instead, your palate will spot notes of cinnamon, nutmeg and clove - spices ‘inspired by the pumpkin season’.

Is a Pumpkin Spice Latte bad for you?

Excessive consumption of any food or drink can be bad for you. A PSL combines sugar, caffeine and whipped cream, so while it’s not exactly one of your five a day, but as an occasional treat, it’s just fine.

How many calories are in a Starbucks Pumpkin Spice Latte?

It's hard to give an exact measure here as the calories will vary with the size, milk choice and amount of whipped cream you go for. As a rough guide, a grande Pumpkin Spice Latte with whipped cream is around 304 calories.

How can I order a low-calorie Pumpkin Spice Latte?

Forget the whipped cream and order the smallest size available for a quick win, then choose almond milk, and your drink will come to about 210 calories.

Why do people get so angry about Pumpkin Spice Lattes?

Coffee connoisseurs are not big fans of the PSL, with many arguing that the sugary syrup ruins the taste of the coffee. The biggest problem people have with it, however, is its sheer ubiquity. Once it returns each autumn, it seems to take over the internet for a few weeks, and you'll likely spot them all over the UK.

Is there a Pumpkin Spice Latte recipe I can try at home?

Yes, you can! You'll need:

  • 2tsp pumpkin puree

  • 2 pinches of ground cinnamon

  • 1 pinch of ground ginger

  • 1 pinch of ground nutmeg

  • 30ml espresso

  • 250ml milk (type is up to you)

  • Whipped cream (optional)

To make your PSL, first mix the pumpkin puree, spices and coffee in a large mug. 

Then, gently heat the milk in a saucepan until steaming and frothy, then pour into the mug and stir. Finally, top with whipped cream (optional), some extra cinnamon and enjoy!
