Judo Bank a ‘victim’ of bot trading, says CEO

Australian digital bank saw shares fall by 46% in June following ‘overreaction’ to bad loans disclosure
by Kimberley Long
Share the article

Australian challenger Judo Bank saw its market value drop by A$800mn following a revised profit forecast © Brendon Thorne/Bloomberg

Investors in Australia’s Judo Bank had an outsized reaction to a reduced profit forecast caused by bad loans earlier this year, according to the bank’s CEO, who blamed the share price fall on bot trading.

Judo Bank, a digital bank which specialises in lending to small and medium-sized companies, saw its share value fall 46 per cent in June after the bank cut its full-year profit guidance due to bad loans.
