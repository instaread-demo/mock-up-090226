Gassed up

Euro zone inflation lifts to 3.3% as energy prices spike

5:59am yesterday

The news: Annual inflation across the euro zone is expected to reach 3.3% in August, according to Eurostat’s flash estimate.

Energy prices rose 14.3% during the month, with natural gas prices reaching their highest level since Russia’s invasion of Ukraine in 2022.

Brent crude traded as high as USD94.39 a barrel during August, while refined products also reached some of their highest levels this year.

Despite the hotter headline reading, core inflation, excluding food and fuel, eased to 2.1% in August from 2.2% in July.

David Powell, an economist at Bloomberg Economics, said the data supported his view that a cooling labour market was limiting the pass through of higher commodity prices.

The context: European Central Bank policymakers are debating whether to raise interest rates at a meeting next week.

At the last meeting, some members argued rates should rise before higher energy costs fuel broader inflation, while others wanted to wait for more evidence of a pass-through.

The source: Eurostat
