Man City eye move for Tottenham target Iliman Ndiaye after Cody Gakpo deal collapses

  • by

    Daniel Ochei
  • 31 August 2026
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:31
Instaread

Manchester City have made contact with Iliman Ndiaye’s camp, according to transfer reporter Fabrizio Romano.

They were closing in on a deal to sign Cody Gakpo, but Liverpool rejected their offer of around £80 million, forcing them to turn to Ndiaye.

Tottenham Hotspur also looked close to signing Ndiaye, but the deal has stalled. 

Man City can now sign Ndiaye, who bagged six goals and three assists in 32 Premier League appearances last term.

He has started the current campaign on fire, bagging an assist in Everton’s Premier League opener against Bournemouth.

His versatility allows him to operate across the frontline, just like Omar Marmoush, who recently left City to join Spurs. 

Ndiaye is a trusted Premier League operator and would be a potent weapon in the hands of new manager Enzo Maresca if he moves to Manchester.

A move to join Maresca might be more appealing than working with Roberto De Zerbi at Tottenham. 

The Italian tactician hung him out to dry at Marseille, and it might be better for the Senegal international to play under a manager who respects his talent.

Man City have completed the signing of Palmeiras winger Allan for £34 million. The pacy right-winger will likely replace Savio in the squad.

Elsewhere, City are focused on signing Chelsea midfielder Enzo Fernandez. The 25-year-old wants to join his former manager at the Etihad Stadium. 

A deal is proving difficult as Chelsea want over £120m to let him leave, while City are trying to negotiate a lower fee.
