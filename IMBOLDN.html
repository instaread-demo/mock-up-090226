Aimé Leon Dore Dresses the Baseball Rain Delay

Three-layer GORE-TEX turns Mets, Yankees, and Dodgers colors into genuinely useful stadium outerwear.

Baseball has elaborate rituals for waiting out weather; the clothing is usually less considered. The Aimé Leon Dore Rain Delay Jacket gives that pause a proper shell, combining MLB team graphics with three-layer GORE-TEX construction.

The initial series covers the Mets, Yankees, and Dodgers without falling back on a standard sideline jacket. Each version uses a distinct color scheme and restrained team marks, letting the weatherproof fabric and simple hooded silhouette carry most of the design. The shell is waterproof, windproof, and breathable, and machine-washable construction makes it practical beyond the stadium.

That material choice is the meaningful part of the collaboration. Licensed sports apparel often stops at logos and palette changes; here, the membrane and fully protective build give the jacket a job.

It sits comfortably between fan gear and the kind of technical outerwear that works on a wet commute or weekend trip. The three clubs also give the release an East Coast–West Coast range without turning it into a sprawling league-wide capsule.

Aimé Leon Dore prices each GORE-TEX Rain Delay Jacket at $695. Mets, Yankees, and Dodgers editions are available now through the brand’s online store in limited seasonal quantities.
