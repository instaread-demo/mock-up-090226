He Dumped His Girlfriend In The Middle Of A Restaurant, Leaving Her To Walk Home Alone In The Pouring Rain

The other day, a high school-aged couple came into the restaurant where TikToker Molly (@miss.molllyyy) worked. The girl seemed excited to be there.

She ordered chocolate milk, and Molly brought the beverage to her before taking their orders. The girl ordered chocolate chip pancakes and fruit, while the guy ordered hummus.

About 20 minutes later, Molly returned with their food. The girl was not there. The guy asked if he could get a takeout box for her food and the check.

At first, Molly didn’t think anything of it because high schoolers are always doing weird stuff.

But the host at the restaurant was friends with this couple, so Molly was able to learn what had happened. Apparently, the guy had broken up with the girl, and she was leaving for college the very next day. They came to the restaurant together, but she left and walked home in the rain.

“At least wait until after the entrees,” said Molly. “Like, wait until the end of the meal. What was his thought process there? I think he thought that this was the better way to do things.”

“I guess it’s better than texting her, but now she’s never going to come to my restaurant again. I’m going to be a trigger for her. Poor girl.”

Several TikTok users shared similar stories in the comments section. They either experienced the breakup themselves or witnessed it happening.

“This has actually happened to me. We ordered food, he broke up with me, I walked out in the rain to his car because he drove me. He got the food boxed up and drove me back. It was awful, but I was 22,” commented one user.

“One night, I was serving, and I had a couple that had to have been like probably 17 or 18, and he broke up with her at the table, and then she proceeded to pay the bill and then had to get a ride home with him. They were arguing about breaking up while I was standing there, trying to drop off their drinks,” shared another.

“My ex took me to my favorite restaurant (that was down the street from my house, and I went to all the time) to break up with me! He also did it before we got our food, so we took it to go. Cherry on top was that it was right before my birthday,” wrote a third.

“I found out my ex-boyfriend was cheating on me because I saw a notification on his phone when he was showing me something. Right after we ordered. Food came over as I was nearly in tears. I left and went to my car, and he had to pay and asked for to-go boxes. I have vowed never to go back to that restaurant since because it was so awkward,” added someone else.
